Privacy Policy

App: Spirit School Qaidabad

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spirit School Qaidabad ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when students, parents/guardians, teachers, and authorized school staff use the app.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used and the information entered by the school, the app may process:

  • Account and contact details, such as name, phone number, email address, and login credentials.
  • Student and guardian details, such as student name, admission or roll number, class, and parent/guardian contact information.
  • School records, such as attendance, examination marks, results, timetable information, and fee/payment records.
  • Profile images or other information voluntarily uploaded through an app feature, if that feature is available.
  • Technical information needed to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the app, where collected by the app or its hosting services.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide school-management services, authenticate users, display relevant student and school records, communicate school-related information, maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Access and Sharing

Student and school records are intended for the relevant user and authorized school personnel according to their roles and permissions. We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by hosting, database, notification, or other service providers only as needed to operate the app, or disclosed when required by law. The school should ensure that access is granted only to authorized personnel.

Storage and Security

Information may be stored on the app's backend servers and database. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information; however, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain school records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. To request deletion of an app account or personal information, contact us at the email below. Some school records may need to be retained by the school where required by law or legitimate school-record obligations.

Children's Information

This app may handle student information, including information about children, for school-management and educational purposes under the school's authority. Parents or guardians may contact the school or the app contact below with questions or requests regarding a student's information.

Permissions

The app should request device permissions only when needed for a feature. If optional permissions are requested, users can manage them through their device settings.

Third-Party Services

The app may rely on backend hosting, database, or platform services to function. Those services may process limited information under their own terms and privacy policies. Any third-party SDKs actually included in the published app should also be disclosed in the app's Google Play Data safety section.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
